“The Age of Discovery”**
Where do we come from?
What are we?
Where are we going?
As the sower must sow
And the plunderer must plunder
So too must we walk this path
Untrodden, pure
To take up the helm 
Of our insatiable and ravenous
Overwhelming and blinding
Tarnishing and Overtaking
Desire for the abyss

“You know, I’ve heard
If we stare at the abyss
Those gleaming, wondrous eyes
The abyss will stare back at you”
